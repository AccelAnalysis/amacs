# Editorial Policy

## New user language

A user-selected **Other** entry becomes a provisional proposal, not an immediate canonical capability. The platform should first suggest preferred labels and aliases. Provisional entries may support keyword discovery, but they cannot independently establish verified capability or satisfy a mandatory RFx gate.

## Editorial dispositions

An editor may:

- map the proposal to an existing concept;
- add it as an alias;
- create a new capability;
- create a new property or credential instead;
- split it into multiple concepts;
- retain it as organization-local wording; or
- reject it as duplicative, promotional, incoherent, or outside scope.

## Promotion signals

Repeated independent use, issuer demand, failed searches, recurring team gaps, domain-review evidence, and authoritative external developments may increase review priority. Frequency alone does not establish semantic quality.

## Capability and credibility separation

AMACS describes what a capability means. RFxchange credibility describes what an organization has established, done, completed, or earned. These systems may reference each other but are not interchangeable.
